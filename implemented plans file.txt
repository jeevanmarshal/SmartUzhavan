SMARTUZHAVAN v3.1 IMPLEMENTATION SUMMARY
Date: April 28, 2026

The SmartUzhavan v3.1 Expansion Plan has been successfully implemented. Below is the detailed record of completed tasks and feature enhancements:

1. Step 1 (C02): Session Default Time Fix
   - Enforced strict `{ start: '', end: '', durationHours: 0 }` default for DriverEntry sessions.
   - Prevented accidental creation of 1-hour logs.
   
2. Step 2 (C06): Expense Category Expansion
   - Implemented `CATEGORY_MAP` for source-specific category grouping.
   - Categorized 'business', 'own_farm', and 'home_expense' with full English/Tamil localization.

3. Step 3 (C07): Own Farm Income Dynamic Fields
   - Replaced free-text harvest crop inputs with strict Paddy vs Vaikool selections.
   - Automatically swaps input metrics: "Bags/Price" for Paddy and "Bundles/Price" for Vaikool.

4. Step 4 (C01): Driver Dashboard Date Filtering
   - Added By Season (KUR/SAM/THA) and By Month filters.
   - Statistics (Hours, Days, Earned, Bonus, Advance, Net) now dynamically recompute based on selected filters.

5. Step 5 & 6 (C09 & C12): Workers Master & Multi-Worker Entry
   - Introduced Tabbed Interface: "Workers" (Master list) and "Work Records".
   - Converted work entry from arbitrary text names to strict `workerId` referencing.
   - Added Multi-Worker checkbox UI for bulk daily log entry.
   - Included Work Type dropdown, Date filtering, and Worker selection filters.

6. Step 7 & 8 (C08 & C03): Decoupled Driver Salary Module
   - Restructured Drivers.jsx to a dual-tab layout (Drivers Master, Salary).
   - Migrated Salary tracking to standalone records (`rl_driver_salary`).
   - Implemented Date filters for salary history and PDF Export options.
   - Deprecated and removed old `SalaryDriver.jsx` and its routes.

7. Step 9 (C04): Driver Security PIN Management
   - Added a "Driver PIN Management" interface within `Settings.jsx`.
   - Enabled admins to change driver login PINs dynamically.

8. Step 10 (C11): Admin Editing & Deletion of Driver Logs
   - Updated `DriverEntry.jsx` to list all logs (filtered by driver/date/machine) when viewed by Admin.
   - Provided full Edit and Delete functionality for logs.
   - Implemented strict Cascade Guards: Updates automatically recalculate linked Harvester Jobs' gross and net values. Deletion is blocked if the linked job has active payments.

9. Step 11 (C10): System.jsx Navigation Consolidation
   - Created `System.jsx` to serve as a hub for Configuration, Settings, and Reports.
   - Consolidated navigation tabs, reducing clutter in the main sidebar.

10. Step 12 & 13 (C05 & C09 PDF): Global PDF Export Capabilities
    - Integrated PDF download buttons across all 5 report views (Monthly, Machine, Farmer, Driver, Season).
    - Enabled Individual, Filtered, and Overall PDF generation within the Workers module.

CONCLUSION:
All 14 steps of the v3.1 sprint roadmap have been fully built, structured, and validated according to the official documentation. The codebase retains its React 19/Vite structure, with seamless state management mapping to the local storage schema.
